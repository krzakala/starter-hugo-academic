The **Cargèse School on Statistical Physics, Machine Learning and Computer Science** is a recurring meeting for physicists, mathematicians, and computer scientists working on spin glasses, inference, neural networks, and learning. I co-organize the series with [Lenka Zdeborová](https://people.epfl.ch/lenka.zdeborova/).

## Editions

- **2027 - The next Cargèse School.** In preparation.
- **2025 - Statistical physics and machine learning: moving forward.** [Visit the archive](https://cargese2025.github.io/).
- **2023 - Statistical physics and machine learning back together again.** [Visit the archive](https://cargese2023.github.io/).
- **2018 - Statistical physics and machine learning back together.** [Visit the archive](https://krzakala.github.io/cargese.io/).
- **2014 - Spin glasses: an old tool for new problems.** Programmes and materials are available through the [series archive](https://cargeseschools.github.io/archive/2014/).
