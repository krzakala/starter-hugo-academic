**LemanTh - the Lausanne Event on Machine Learning and Neural Network Theory** brings together researchers working on the mathematical foundations of modern machine learning. The meeting connects machine learning theory with high-dimensional statistics, probability, statistical physics, optimization, and theoretical computer science. I organize the series with [Bruno Loureiro](https://brloureiro.github.io/).
