I have organized several schools and workshops at the **Ecole de Physique des Houches**, bringing together researchers and students around statistical physics, inference, optimization, message-passing algorithms, and machine learning.

## Editions

### 2022 - Summer School on Statistical Physics of Machine Learning

The school originally planned for 2020 was held in July 2022. Its website collects the programme, speakers, and lecture material.

[Visit the 2022 school website](https://leshouches2022.github.io/)

### 2020 - Summer Workshop on Statistical Physics and Machine Learning

When the full school had to be postponed, a smaller workshop was held from 3-14 August 2020. The original website and material are preserved here:
